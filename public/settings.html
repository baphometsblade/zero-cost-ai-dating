Connect your own Firebase project

Checking which mode this browser is in…

Paste the web config from your Firebase console (Project settings → Your apps → Config) as JSON. It is saved in this browser only, under the localStorage key zc.firebaseConfig, and the app reads it on the next load — so you can leave demo mode without editing a single file.

Needs apiKey, authDomain, projectId and appId; storageBucket and messagingSenderId are optional. Quote every key and value, JSON-style.

A Firebase web config is not a secret — it identifies your project, it does not authorise anything. What protects your data is the Firestore rules shipped in this repository.

Your data

Export my data

Downloads a JSON file with your profile, your swipes, your matches and your messages. Other people's profiles are reduced to a name and an id — this is your export, not theirs.

Reset the demo data

Wipes the browser database and re-seeds the bundled sample profiles. Your swipes, matches and messages go with it. Handy when you have swiped through everyone.

Sign out

Ends the session in this browser.

Delete my account

Removes your profile document, every swipe you have made and every match you are part of, along with those conversations. This is permanent and there is no undo.

The button below stays disabled until this box reads exactly DELETE.
