      How the simulation works

      Switching plans sets plan to premium or free on your own user document. Before every like, super like or rewind, the app asks its own store whether that plan has budget left today; that is the whole of the gating logic, and it is all running in this tab.

      Because the check happens in your browser, it is a demonstration of the mechanism rather than an enforcement of it — anyone who can open the developer tools can flip the same field. That is honest and deliberate: this app has no server, and a paywall you cannot enforce is not one worth pretending about.

      The app costs $0 a month to run: static hosting, the free Firestore tier, and a matching engine that never leaves your device. There is nothing to fund.
