How it works

Three steps, and the middle one is the whole product.

1

Tell it who you are

A short bio, up to twelve interests, five personality sliders, your city and how far you are willing to travel. That is the entire input — no quiz, no questionnaire, no tracking.

2

The engine ranks everyone

TF-IDF vectors over bios compared with cosine similarity, weighted Jaccard over interest tags, per-axis personality scoring, great-circle distance, age and recency — combined into one score and turned into readable reasons. It runs locally, in milliseconds.

3

Swipe, match, actually talk

Match with someone and you get icebreakers built from what the two of you genuinely share, not a blank box and a shrug. Keyboard shortcuts work everywhere the drag gestures do.

Straight answers

Is it actually AI?

It is machine learning, not a large language model: term-frequency vectors, cosine similarity and weighted scoring — the maths recommendation systems ran on for years. That is a deliberate trade. It is deterministic, instant, explainable line by line, and it costs nothing per request. No prompt is ever sent anywhere.

Is it really free, or free-for-now?

Free by architecture. The app is deliberately built inside the always-free tiers: static hosting, the Firestore free quota, no Cloud Functions, no Cloud Storage, no paid API. There is no metered service to switch on later.

What happens to my data?

In demo mode it never leaves your browser — profiles, swipes, matches and messages all live in localStorage, and clearing your site data deletes them. Connect your own Firebase project and it lives in your Firestore, under rules that let only you write your own document.

Is Premium a real payment?

No, and the upgrade screen says so before you click. Premium flips a local flag so the daily limits, rewinds and "who liked you" gating can be tried out. No card details are collected and no payment is processed.

Can I point it at my own Firebase project?

Yes. Paste your web config into Settings and the app switches from demo mode to your project on the next load — no files to edit, no redeploy.

Why can't I upload photos?

Cloud Storage needs a paid plan on new Firebase projects, so uploads were cut. Instead every profile gets a distinctive gradient avatar generated from its name, and you can add up to six https image URLs of your own.
